Improper Waste Disposal Awareness Campaign

"More Than Just One Piece of Trash"

CHARACTERS:

Nico - a student who does not think littering is a serious problem

Aira - Nico's classmate who cares about the environment

Mrs. Santos - their teacher

Mang Leo - a community sanitation worker

Narrator

Scene 1: The Problem-The Careless Choice

(The scene takes place outside the school during recess. Students are eating snacks near the school gate.)

Narrator: It was an ordinary afternoon at school. Students were eating, talking, and enjoying their break. However, small pieces of garbage were already scattered around the area.

Nico: I'm finished with my drink.

(Nico throws a plastic bottle beside a bench.)

Aira: Nico, the garbage bin is just a few steps away.

Nico: It is only one bottle. It will not make a big difference.

Aira: Maybe one bottle seems small, but what if everyone thinks the same way?

Nico: Someone will clean it later.

(Mrs. Santos walks past and notices the bottle.)

Mrs. Santos: Keeping the environment clean is not only the responsibility of the people who clean it. It is also our responsibility as members of the community.

Scene 2: The Conflict-The Consequences of Littering

(Later that day, dark clouds appear. Heavy rain begins to fall.)

Narrator: That afternoon, heavy rain fell over the community. The water flowed toward the drainage, carrying wrappers, bottles, and plastic bags with it.

Mang Leo: The drainage is blocked again. We need to remove this garbage before the water rises.

Aira: The water is already entering the street.

Nico: Is this really because of the litter?

Mang Leo: Yes. Garbage thrown on the ground can be carried into the drainage. When the water cannot pass through, flooding happens.

Nico: I never thought my bottle could become part of a problem like this.

Mrs. Santos: Environmental problems are often caused by many small careless actions. That is why responsible choices matter.

Scene 3: The Realization-It Does Not Simply Disappear

(The rain becomes lighter. The characters look at the flooded street.)

Nico: I used to think that one piece of trash was not important. Now I understand that every piece adds to the problem when people refuse to take responsibility.

*Nico's mind*: I realized that the garbage I threw away did not really disappear. It went somewhere, and its effects were felt by people who did nothing wrong.

Aira: The environment is not separate from us. The water we pollute and the places we dirty are the same places where we live.

Nico: I am sorry. I should have put the bottle in the proper bin instead of expecting someone else to clean it.

Mang Leo: Recognizing the problem is the first step. The next step is choosing to do something about it.

Scene 4: Positive Action-The Change Begins

(The next morning, the students and community members join a clean-up activity.)

Nico: I will collect the plastic bottles and place them in the recycling bin.

Aira: I will separate the food waste from the recyclable materials.

Mrs. Santos: We can also bring reusable bottles and containers to reduce the amount of waste we produce.

Mang Leo: If we dispose of waste properly and keep the drainage clear, we can help prevent flooding.

Nico: I will also remind my friends that throwing garbage anywhere affects more than just the place where it lands.

Ending/Call to Action-A Cleaner Future Starts with Us

(The area is clean. The characters hold a sign that says, "The Earth Is Our Home.")

Narrator: Waste does not disappear when we throw it away. It remains in our streets, waterways, soil, and communities. The condition of our environment reflects the choices we make every day.

Nico: Do not wait for a flood or a dirty community before you decide to act.

Aira: Use the proper bins, reduce unnecessary waste, and encourage others to do the same.

Mrs. Santos: A responsible community begins with responsible people.

All: The Earth is our home. Let us protect it, not turn it into a trash can.

"A clean environment begins with one responsible choice."

-By Steff Ainsley Nicolas(Individual)
