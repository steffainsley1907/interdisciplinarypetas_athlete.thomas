Living the Values of St. Dominic in Our Community

As students of Concepcion Catholic School, we are called to live out the values of St. Dominic not only inside the classroom but also in our homes and community. Our values should guide us in responding to environmental and social problems.

Truth means being honest and responsible with the information we share. We should avoid spreading fake news, especially on social media, and learn more about issues such as climate change, pollution, poverty, and discrimination. By speaking the truth respectfully, we can help others understand the real problems in our society.

Service can be shown through simple actions in school and in our barangay. We can keep our surroundings clean, follow proper waste segregation, conserve water and electricity, and participate in community activities. We can also help classmates and neighbors who need support without expecting anything in return.

Compassion reminds us to be sensitive to the feelings and situations of other people. Instead of judging someone who is poor, bullied, or struggling, we should listen to them and offer help. We can also show compassion by protecting animals, planting trees, and taking care of the environment that provides for everyone.

Peacebuilding begins with the way we treat one another. As students, we can avoid bullying, respect different opinions, settle misunderstandings calmly, and include those who often feel left out. We should choose kindness and understanding instead of spreading rumors or starting arguments.

Faith in Action means putting our beliefs into practice. Praying is important, but our faith becomes stronger when it leads us to do what is right. We can show our faith by standing against injustice, helping people in need, caring for creation, and encouraging others to become responsible citizens.

As learners of Concepcion Catholic School, we may still be young, but we are capable of making a difference. By practicing these values every day, we can help create a school and community that are cleaner, more peaceful, fairer, and more caring. The change may begin with small actions, but when we do them together, they can have a lasting effect.
